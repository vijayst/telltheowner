# TellTheOwner - Competitive Analysis & Market Positioning

## Executive Summary

TellTheOwner operates in a fragmented customer feedback market valued at **$15.23B (2026)** and projected to reach **$33.14B by 2031** (16.82% CAGR). The platform uniquely combines **voice recording** with **QR code deployment** and **private-first feedback** - a niche currently underserved by competitors.

**Key Market Insights:**
- QR code adoption for feedback surged **323% between 2021-2024**
- 73% of businesses use QR codes; only 26% link them to feedback
- Voice feedback is **3x faster** than text but adoption remains low
- 40-60% response rate for QR codes vs 15-25% for email surveys
- Small businesses ($9-39/month price sensitivity) represent largest addressable market

**Competitive Position:** TellTheOwner sits between specialized voice platforms (Hearback), QR survey tools (FeedbackRobot), and enterprise VoC platforms (Zonka Feedback). No dominant player owns the **voice + QR + private feedback** combination.
